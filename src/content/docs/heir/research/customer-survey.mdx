The sample contains **100 existing HEIR customers**. Overall usefulness was rated **4 or 5 by 53%** of respondents.

<StatisticalPlot src={chart0} alt="Overall usefulness of HEIR" />

## What customers primarily value

<StatisticalPlot src={chart1} alt="Calls and app alerts are the most common primary source of value" />

**58%** selected calls/app alerts, silent alarms, or reminders as their primary value. This is a sample majority, but it does not clearly exceed a 50% population benchmark at the conventional threshold (**p = 0.067**).

## Where customers experience difficulty

<StatisticalPlot src={chart2} alt="Alert clarity is the most common individual pain point" />

Overall, **53%** reported at least one alert-specific difficulty: missed alerts, unclear alerts, or excessive alerts.

## What customers want improved

<StatisticalPlot src={chart3} alt="Notification controls dominate the improvement list" />

**75%** requested at least one improvement to app-alert selection, vibration behavior, quiet hours, alarms, or reminders. The exact one-sided test against 50% gives **p &lt;0.001**. A notification-system item was the single top improvement for **49%**.

## A useful behavioral segmentation

<StatisticalPlot src={chart4} alt="Notification-improvement demand is highest when alert friction is present" />

Customers with alert friction requested notification-system improvements at **85%**, compared with **64%** among customers without alert friction. The association is statistically significant (**odds ratio = 3.1, p = 0.021**).

## Open-ended feedback

<StatisticalPlot src={chart5} alt="Open comments reinforce vibration and notification-control needs" />

The open comments are consistent with the closed-ended results: vibration clarity/strength, notification control, and alarm/reminder flexibility recur alongside settings, activity accuracy, battery, health interpretation, and connection issues. These counts are descriptive theme indicators, not formal qualitative coding with inter-rater reliability.

## Readout

The survey supports prioritizing **notification selection, vibration refinement, quiet-period controls, and alarm/reminder flexibility**. The strongest evidence is not simply that notifications are the only reason customers use HEIR; it is that notification-system improvements are requested broadly, and especially by customers already experiencing alert friction.
