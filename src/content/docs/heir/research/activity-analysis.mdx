The dataset contains **847 users** across **21,258 user-days**. Users contribute a median of **26 observed days**, leaving natural variation in how much data each person contributes.

## How often is the app used?

<StatisticalPlot src={chart0} alt="Most users open the app on only a few days" />

The median user opened the app on **2 days**. **12%** never opened it during the observation window, while **12%** opened it on eight or more days.

## Do users explore activity beyond Home?

<StatisticalPlot src={chart1} alt="Insights attract a minority of users" />

**29%** opened Insights at least once. Deeper exploration was less common: **13%** used a longer timeframe and **8%** opened the Pace explanation.

## How attainable is Adaptive Target?

<StatisticalPlot src={chart2} alt="Adaptive Target is reached intermittently, not routinely" />

Across eligible user-days, the Adaptive Target was reached **40%** of the time. The median user reached it on **40%** of eligible days.

<StatisticalPlot src={chart3} alt="Target attainment is similar when Home is actually viewed" />

Attainment was **40.6%** on days when Home was viewed versus **40.0%** across all eligible days. The difference is small.

## How long do target streaks last?

<StatisticalPlot src={chart4} alt="Most users build short target streaks" />

The most common longest streak was **4–5 days**. Only **11%** sustained a streak of six days or longer.

## Did activity change across the month?

<StatisticalPlot src={chart5} alt="Average daily steps rise gradually across the four weeks" />

Among **789 users** with sufficient data in all four weeks, mean daily steps rose from **5,646** to **6,021**: an average increase of **375 steps/day**. **59%** increased, and the paired comparison is statistically significant (**p &lt;0.001**).

## Is heavier app use associated with target success?

<StatisticalPlot src={chart6} alt="Opening the app more often does not strongly separate target success" />

Target-attainment rates are similar across app-use groups. The differences are not statistically clear (**Kruskal–Wallis p = 0.5**).

## What does tracked movement look like?

<StatisticalPlot src={chart7} alt="Most defensible tracked time is sedentary" />

Across defensible tracked time, **69%** was sedentary, **27%** casual, and **4%** active.

## Readout

HEIR behaves like a **low-touch companion app**: most users open it only occasionally, while a smaller group actively explores Insights. Adaptive Target is reached on about **40% of eligible days**, with mostly short streaks rather than constant success. Activity rises modestly across the month, but heavier app use is **not** strongly associated with higher target attainment.
